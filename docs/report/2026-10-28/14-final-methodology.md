# 14 — Final Methodology Proposal: PASIPHAE

Working title **PASIPHAE** — *Proof-Anchored Single-truth Iterative-hardening Fault-Aware Evidence-grounded architecture*. Proposal only: `METHODOLOGY.md` is read-only ground truth (ADR-0005); every delta below routes through the ADR-ready change list (§7). Structure retained from THESEUS: 7-phase Harel statechart macro-shell, orthogonal per-module regions, typed two-tier blackboard, deterministic engine + on-demand arbiter, VAL-* contracts.

## 2. The portfolio-race solver (replaces uniform-UCB rollout spending)

Per vertex $v_i$, strategies $h_1..h_K$ from $\mathcal{A}_{\text{str}}$ race before any repair investment:

1. **Race phase.** Each active strategy receives paired evaluation rounds on **short repair rollouts at a fixed probe depth $d = 2$** (not zero-shot drafts — RefuteThe F1 — and not depth-1, whose myopic horizon re-introduces the same bias in the other direction: principled multi-turn strategies need $d \ge 2$ to clear cross-function signature repairs, RecheckTheory W2-F4): generate → repair once → layered-oracle evaluate → record outcome vector (compile, pass, oracle-strength, differential-parity, receipt id). Strategies are eliminated by successive elimination with paired statistics against the current leader; the race ends when one strategy dominates on the paired outcome vectors or the race budget is exhausted. An exploration floor $N_{\min}$ **per strategy arm, per race** (scope fixed per RecheckTheory W2-F6) is guaranteed before any elimination may fire (RefuteThe F3: stronger oracles make rewards sparser; gating and exploration budgets are decoupled). Cost bound: with $K \le 3$ racing arms and $N_{\min} = 2$ depth-2 probes (≈2.4K output tokens/arm), the floor spends ≤ ~7K tokens ≈ 26 s per race — bounded by and deducted from the vertex's sub-budget, never on top of it. L2 differential probes run on a bounded input subset *during* the race, **only for candidates that already pass L0** (RecheckTheory W2-F7: non-compiling candidates yield zero differential signal; dual-binary execution is reserved for compiling candidates so the probe stays cheap), and only at the final race round (not every rollout).
2. **Commit.** The winning strategy's best candidate $c^\star$ becomes the root of a repair-focused search (existing MCTS machinery, retained): $\mathcal{A}_{\text{patch}}$ rollouts with fault records from $\mathcal{B}_{PR}$, RethinkMCTS-style in-place strategy refinement on block-level feedback, AlphaCodium-style **test anchors** (once a suite item passes on the committed candidate, later edits in this subtree must preserve it — oracle weakening is structurally rejected).
3. **Budget accounting.** A dispatch is *futile* only if it produced zero information beyond the progress quantum: no reward gain of at least one characterization test's worth ($\Delta R \ge 0.6/N_{\text{suite}}$, absolute — RecheckMeas W2-F4), no oracle-stage advance, no new distinguishing test, no eliminated strategy. Information-bearing dispatches reset the futile counter (F7 fix), bounded at $N_{\text{reset\_max}} = 3$ resets per vertex (RefuteThe F7 / RefuteScale F4: unquantified resets permit infinite futile-cap evasion via ε-jitter). $B_{\max}$ per dispatch scales with the unit's difficulty class (R10): synthesis-sized (D5: whole-library rewrites) get an order of magnitude more than patch-sized; the derived wall-clock sub-budget overrides all counters.

**Why not softmax-UCB rollout dispersion:** under a fixed oracle, rewards are deterministic-but-possibly-wrong (oracle defects, LitVerify #21) — extra rollouts re-confirm the same wrong value; oracle strengthening fixes that defect class. Separately (RefuteLit F4 / RefuteThe F3, disentangled): *candidate sampling is genuinely stochastic* — empirical Bernoulli outcomes across rollouts do support confidence-bound reasoning — but UCT's theoretical guarantees still assume stationary i.i.d. arms, and repair makes strategy arms non-stationary, so no regret bound is claimed (Coquelin & Munos: broken assumptions can make UCT exponentially optimistic). Race-then-commit is justified as an empirical compute-budget heuristic matching the SR+CR two-phase shape (Tolpin & Shimony): spend exploration on *distinguishing strategies under repair*, then commit the remaining budget to the winner. **Stall escape (RefuteThe F1/F4; interplay fixed per RecheckTheory W2-F3):** if the committed winner shows $\Delta R < 0.6/N_{\text{suite}}$ for $K_{\text{stall}}$ rollouts (with $K_{\text{stall}} < B_{\max}$ and the stall counter persisting across dispatches so the futile cap cannot outrun it), `ev_StrategyStalled` demotes it and revives the runner-up from checkpoint (≤2 revivals per vertex). A stall-triggered demotion is **not** a strategy elimination for budget purposes (it resets no futile counter — otherwise failure would buy budget, RecheckTheory W2-F3.2). After both revivals stall, the vertex exhausts its portfolio: further dispatches count futile normally and the vertex reaches `M_Failed(StrategyExhaustion)` at the cap — no third path. Race restarts happen only on *external* oracle changes (see §3), never on intra-module gate advances.

**Totality rules (T1/T8 fixes; deadlock-closed per RefuteThe F5; micro-state semantics per RecheckTheory W2-F1):** all strategies eliminated ⇒ `SolverOutcome::ExhaustedStrategies`; the micro-FSM increments the futile dispatch counter; **below the cap**, the module parks in its existing parked micro-state (M0-parked, the same parentless-park state — re-entering the dispatchable set is exactly what ReadySet recomputation would re-dispatch into a vacuous loop) with a `strategies_exhausted: true` marker and **excluded from ReadySet** until an external unblocking event (new strategy hypothesis from blackboard evidence, oracle upgrade, upstream invalidation) re-arms it — **parking is permitted only while a live unblock source exists** (an upstream dispatch in flight, or a non-empty blackboard hypothesis queue); with no live unblock source the module transitions directly to terminal `M_Failed(StrategyExhaustion)` at park time (wave-3 W3-1: a parked-unarmed below-cap module is invisible to the {M1–M3} liveness invariant and deadlocks the scheduler) — this is the R3 parentless-park semantics, not a new eighth state; **at the cap**, it transitions to terminal `M_Failed(StrategyExhaustion)` emitting `ev_ModuleFailed(v_i)`. **Failure cascade (macro side):** `ev_ModuleFailed(v_i)` deterministically marks every downstream-dependent module `Failed(DependencyUnmet)` without dispatch (children never enter the dispatchable set waiting on a failed parent — closing the S5-hang hole RecheckTheory W2-F1.2); sibling branches whose entire downstream closure is terminal are cancelled at their next micro-state boundary without further spend (wave-3 W3-8 diamond fix); `Failed(DependencyUnmet)` is recoverable only via a Tier-B/C regression event carrying a proof object (R12 re-entry, deterministic — no silent revival); the campaign receipt reports both the failed vertex and its transitively-unmet dependents. It never parks in M0 *with satisfied parents* (that would re-dispatch forever) and never idles in the dispatchable set (that would trip the liveness invariant into a false-positive). Assembly-contract failure ⇒ intrinsic fault owned by the *slice* whose contract broke; parentless budget exhaustion ⇒ park + futile-count, never Tier-B (VAL-M2-05 generalized).

## 3. The layered oracle (replaces trust-the-pass-rate)

Ordered gates; each layer runs only if the previous passed (cost monotonicity):

- **L0 structural** (deterministic, zero-token): compiles against parent artifacts; zero stubs; assembly contracts hold.
- **L1 suite strength** (deterministic, cheap): the characterization suite must prove sensitivity before its pass rate counts — **stub-sensitivity is mandatory on every suite** (suite must fail against implementation deletion; cost honesty per RecheckScale W2-F2: the wave-1 "1.4%" figure holds only for interpreted single-function modules — coarse whole-file stubbing is one run per suite (~100 s interpreted, up to ~17% of $W$ for compiled crates at 5–10 s incremental builds), and fine-grained per-symbol stubbing is reserved for the mutation-audit tier, not the mandatory gate); **mutation kills are a campaign-level spot audit** (≤5 modules per campaign, ≤30-mutant sample each, 10 s per-mutant timeout — never per-module or per-candidate; per-candidate mutation is 6.7× the campaign bound and per-suite mutation across 100 modules is 50 min–8.3 h, RefuteScale W2-F1; the audit calibrates trust in the suite *generator*: a failed audit escalates to mandatory amplification for that generator's outputs and receipt-labels its prior hardens `stub_sensitivity_only`); campaigns that skip the audit carry the same label; error-path + call-sequence coverage from the legacy execution traces. Suite-strength receipt stored in $\mathcal{B}_{CT}$ metadata (TestGenEval: mutation score is the honest adequacy metric; coverage as cheap proxy).
- **L2 differential execution** (deterministic): candidate vs legacy on shared inputs from legacy traces + generated edge cases (incl. adversarial inputs from the ACToR pattern where binaries exist). Parity rate, not LLM judgment, is the equivalence signal (EquiBench: models cannot judge equivalence). **Gate threshold (wave-3 W3-6):** exact match on `require_byte_identical_io: true` configs; ≥0.95 normalized parity otherwise, with the normalization (whitespace, float formatting) documented per receipt — silent tolerances are the D2 defect class; for non-executable legacy, L4 verdict aggregation substitutes and the receipt labels `parity: verdict_only`.
- **L3 property invariants** (LLM-assisted, bounded): behavioral invariants extracted from rule cards (idempotence, inverses, error contracts) checked on both sides (PBT: +23–37% over example-only oracles).
- **L4 verdict aggregation** (LLM, only where execution impossible): MatchFixAgent-pattern multi-dimension reports → verdict; never the sole basis for hardening when L2 was possible.

Oracle upgrades (new/amplified tests, stronger gates) **restart the race** for affected units — the reward distribution of every arm shifts, so pre-upgrade eliminations are voided (RefuteThe F1's non-stationarity handled by restart, not by pretending stationarity).

## 4. Single-ground-truth receipts (G4/R1)

The harness adapter (§5) produces one receipt object per execution: build status, per-test results in target-native format, stub census, oracle-strength enum (`stub_sensitivity_only | stub_plus_mutation_audit | full`), exercised-claims array (`unexercised: [claim_id]` — schema-level, auditable, R13), wall-clock, provenance. All consumers — evaluator, reward computation, hardening events, campaign acceptance, reports — read the same receipt. The cluster-internal reward $R(s_t)$ keeps its form but its terms are receipt fields; `ev_ModuleHardened(v_i, c^\star, receipt_id)` is invalid without a receipt reference (VAL extension).

## 5. Harness adapter as config plane (G6/R6)

```rust
trait TargetHarness {
    fn build(&self, ws: &Workspace) -> BuildReceipt;
    fn test(&self, ws: &Workspace) -> TestReceipt;      // per-test granularity, target-native format
    fn receipt_schema(&self) -> ReceiptSchema;           // versioned, provenance-tagged
    fn bounds(&self) -> ExecBounds;                      // build/test/inference timeouts, wall-clock
}
```

Consumes `stack.target_build_cmd/target_test_cmd` (already in BenchConfig, ADR-0009, currently unconsumed); skel's `node -c`/`node translated.js` becomes a first-class JS harness; `require_byte_identical_io` maps to the L2 differential gate. Wall-clock bounds enforced at the harness layer — the 2 h campaign bound becomes a *policy* with defined truncation semantics (§6), not a post-hoc observation.

## 6. Termination, liveness, and graceful degradation (F1/F2/toml fixes)

- **Outcome totality**: every solver dispatch ends in exactly one of {Hardened(receipt), Progress(info), BudgetExhausted{anchored_on: Option<Edge>}, ExhaustedStrategies, Invalid(report)}; only a real edge anchors Tier-B; parentless exhaustion parks (M0) and counts futile; invalid reports park their reporter (VAL-MACRO-17/18 promoted to spec).
- **Liveness invariant** (VAL-checkable): at every scheduler tick, ∄ module in {M1,M2,M3} **∪ {M0-held}** (parked with `strategies_exhausted`, or blocked behind a parked/failed ancestor) while ReadySet=∅ and event queue empty ⇒ **Unreachable sweep**: transitively classify every M0-held vertex and its dependents `Failed(StrategyExhaustion)`/`Failed(DependencyUnmet)` (receipt-visible), recompute; if non-terminal modules still remain with empty ReadySet and queue ⇒ terminal ModuleRetryExhausted with full state dump (wave-3 W3-1).
- **Campaign wall-clock budget**: total budget $W$ (default 2 h) partitioned up front: discovery phases capped, per-unit budgets derived from difficulty class and remaining $W$ as *zero-sum fractions of the ~16-step campaign mean* (RecheckScale W2-F4). **Difficulty-tier admission (wave-3 W3-5):** tier assignment is a deterministic blackboard predicate (unit LoC, stub density D5, binding-closure size, fan-in) computed in S2; the hard tier (40–64 steps) is quota-capped at $\lceil 0.15 \cdot |V| \rceil$ vertices prioritized by fan-in × coupling score (at 100 modules the 1,608-step envelope affords ≤ ~10 hard vertices plus ~70 patch-tier vertices); mid-campaign escalation requires evicting the lowest-priority scheduled hard vertex or downgrading the escalated vertex to its binding-closure core; a patch-tier vertex showing ≥2 one-test progress gains within its 8 steps earns one 8-step extension (progress-earned, charged to its reset allowance) — heapq-class vertices converge or terminalize honestly rather than false-failing at step 8; every unit carries a derived wall-clock sub-budget whose exhaustion terminalizes it regardless of futile-cap reset counters (single pathological modules cannot consume >~40% of $W$). On $W$ exhaustion: a pre-reserved tail budget $W_{\text{tail}} = \min(5\text{ min}, 2\%W)$ runs the final harness pass on best-known candidates; a non-terminal module scores accept **only if its existing receipt chain already satisfies the full hardened-accept predicate** — no acceptance evidence is invented at truncation time; others classify partial-credit with their best oracle layer (`CampaignTimeout{units: [per-unit status]}`) — never a fabricated pass, never a 17.1 h runaway. Full truncation semantics (`10-meta-strategy.md` §5b): truncated campaigns are measurements, not invalid runs.

## 8. What PASIPHAE does *not* fix

- Dataset validity (D5–D8, G8): the corpus's oracle-relative success rates and zero SCC stress are properties of ReCodeAgent's release; PASIPHAE's claims on it remain oracle-relative and Tier C remains validation-contract-validated only. A curated multi-module SCC-bearing corpus stays future work.
- Frontier-model equivalence: PASIPHAE is designed for the 27B regime; results are not transferable claims about larger models (and vice versa).
- Package-manager dependency surface (D7, reclassified out of scope per RefuteCover F1): third-party manifest/dependency translation is a declared non-goal; projects requiring it classify `dependency_untranslated` partial-credit.
